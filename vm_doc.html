The View-Master Documentary

Written and Produced by Dave Machin


Watch Now

I spent nine months independently writing, narrating, animating, and editing this documentary to tell the complete, untold story of View-Master.

Along the way, I interviewed original experts, dug up forgotten FBI files and SEC records, and reconstructed the actual physical techniques used to build these 3D reels.

If you want an honest, deep-dive breakdown of the brilliant people and chaotic corporate drama behind the viewer, this video is for you.

What's in the documentary

  • How 3D vision actually works — starting with Leonardo Da Vinci's observations in the 1500s and Charles Wheatstone's first mirror stereoscope.
  • How player pianos inspired View-Master — using pneumatic valves and hole-punched cardboard concepts to design the reel system.
  • The chance meeting at Oregon Caves — where William Gruber pitched his wild idea to Sawyer's postcard company president, Harold Graves.
  • View-Master's public debut at the 1939 New York World's Fair — where it instantly captured everyone's imagination.
  • Handcrafted 3D fairy tale worlds built by artist Florence Thomas — who spent weeks sculpting detailed 1/6th-scale clay models.
  • The secret WWII FBI investigation into Gruber — who was suspected of being a German spy over a mysterious bank wire transfer.
  • Top-secret military training reels used during World War II — to help troops identify enemy aircraft and ships.
  • A wild 35mm camera system designed by Stereocraft — that let everyday families shoot and mount their own View-Master reels at home.
  • Behind-the-scenes TV photos — taken right on set during production for hit shows like The Man From U.N.C.L.E.
  • How fake 3D collages were made — by slicing up flat 2D movie press slides and shifting them using high-contrast film masks.
  • A massive corporate rivalry — where GAF bought Sawyer's in 1966 to use View-Master as a heavy hitter against Kodak.
  • Hand-drawn 3D cartoons — created by artists who freehanded the right-eye view and checked depth using stereo viewers, including an interview with artist John Kurtz.
  • The dark final chapter of the Portland plant — including a decades-long groundwater cleanup caused by cleaning solvents.

AI & Image Restoration

The image of women assembling “model B” viewers has been colorized. The letter from the FBI to William Gruber is quoted from the FBI file, but the letter is generated since there is no image of it.

AI images include the image of the FBI interview room, the images with television sets in them (although what's on the TV was added by me), a background image of a drive-in, and some other minor image assets.

The initial gunfighter “coming alive” was AI generated. Restored color of View-Master images was AI.
